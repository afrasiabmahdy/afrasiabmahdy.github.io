About the developer

One developer.
Many ideas.

Built independently from concept to release, Afghan Game Developer focuses on simple, entertaining experiences with an Afghan identity. The goal is straightforward: create games that are fun to pick up, memorable to play, and representative of a community that deserves to see more of itself in games.
